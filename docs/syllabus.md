# Syllabus

**Course:** UPER mastery  
**Student path:** daily practitioner training  
**End date:** 15 December 2026

## Contract

- One lesson per day, 30–40 minutes.
- Listen, then write, then reflect, then ask.
- Marks are 0 / 1 / 2 against the [rubric](rubric.md).
- Voice lessons should be lively enough to listen to — still calm and firm, not monotone.
- If you miss days, say so. Catch up at one lesson per day.

## What “mastered” means

You can do the list in the root README without opening the methodology repo. Evidence lives on the mastery checklist in the teaching workbook, not as a public leaderboard.

## Weekly arc

1. Foundation — field card, charter, roles, control buckets, language hygiene  
2–3. Understand — statements, facts, criteria, scope, Owner lock, Challenger question  
4. Plan — two options, gates, stop rules, reference class  
5. Execute — evidence, deviations, last mile  
6. Review — then vs now, owned actions  
7. Psychology — traps mapped to stages  
8. Team dynamics — dissent, escalation, role anti-patterns  
9. Facilitation — 15 / 30 / 60 and interrupt drills  
10. Northshore pack — charter / scope fork  
11. Harborline × BrightCart — timeline vs risk, prefer vs demand  
12. Assess others — rubric fluency  
13. Capstone — live brief, plan, two scored cases, teach-back  

Lesson titles: [calendar.md](calendar.md).
