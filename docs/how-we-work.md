# How we work

## Marking

Artefacts are marked **0 / 1 / 2**.

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0 | Missing or theatre (templates filled after the fix) |
| 1 | Partial |
| 2 | Meets the bar |

Effort and seniority are not marks. A lucky heroic save can still score low on process. A clean process that missed can still score on process.

## Honesty rules

- Live work is allowed. Strip secrets, hostnames, and personal data before anything is written in a public place.
- Psychology content stays work-design. Name traps. Do not diagnose colleagues.
- If a legal, HR, IR, or safety process applies, that process leads. UPER does not invent a parallel workshop.
- The Facilitator does not become Owner by being helpful.

## What does not belong in this public repo

- Filled homework with real incident detail
- Scored cases with identifiable initials and context
- Voice MP3s until a binary-safe publish path exists

Keep those in the private teaching workspace or a private fork.
