# Coaching questions

Ask these. Do not donate the fix.

| Stage | Question | Weak answer sounds like |
|-------|----------|-------------------------|
| Any | What stage are you in? | We are just getting on with it. |
| Understand | What is the problem in one or two sentences — not the fix? | We need to implement X. |
| Understand | What do you observe, with a source and a time? | Everyone knows this is broken. |
| Understand | What would solved look like that we could test? | Make it go away / leadership happy. |
| Understand | What is out of scope this cycle? | Silence, or everything they mentioned. |
| Understand | Who is the named Owner? | Security. The team. IMO. |
| Understand | What do you control, influence, cannot control? | A plan step that is actually weather or another org. |
| Understand | What would disprove this framing? | Nothing — we already know. |
| Plan | What is the second approach, and why reject the first alternative? | There is only one obvious way. |
| Plan | When do you stop this plan? | We will see how it goes. |
| Plan | Last similar effort actually took how long? | Half a day if we hustle. |
| Execute | What changed in the world, not in the calendar? | We had a lot of meetings. |
| Execute | What deviation did you take, and who approved it? | We just tweaked it while we were in there. |
| Review | What did you know then versus now? | Obviously we should have… |
| Review | What action is owned, or why is no change needed? | Lessons learned. (No name. No date.) |
| Hygiene | Are you escalating a decision, or venting? | Please help / FYI to twelve people. |
