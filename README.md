# UPER course

**Understand · Plan · Execute · Review**

A 93-day mastery course in psychology-aware team problem solving. Companion to the methodology suite: [danieldejager/uper-team-methodology](https://github.com/danieldejager/uper-team-methodology).

**Window:** 14 September 2026 – 15 December 2026  
**Cadence:** one lesson per day, about 30–40 minutes  
**Tone:** calm, corporate, firm. No guru hype. No therapy claims. No blame framework.

This repository holds the **student course** (syllabus, calendar, rubric, coaching questions).  
The other repository holds the **method** (charter, templates, facilitator packs).

---

## What this course trains

By 15 December you should be able to:

- Name the UPER stage you are in, and the red flag that you are skipping one
- Produce an Owner-locked problem brief or a clean escalation note
- Lock a plan with at least two approaches, named human owners, a stop condition, and a reference class
- Hold Execute to evidence and logged deviations
- Run a lookback that separates information-then from information-now
- Interrupt premature closure, fixation, and absolute language without diagnosing people
- Score how well **someone else** understands a problem, and how well they could or did solve it — without becoming their Owner

Authoring the method is not the same skill as running it under heat. This course trains the practitioner.

## Daily shape

| Block | Minutes | What you do |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| Listen | 8–12 | Voice lesson (generated in the teaching thread) |
| Homework | 15–20 | Written artefact, interrupt script, or scored case |
| Reflect | 5 | Three short questions |
| Ask | as needed | Same thread — mark is 0 / 1 / 2 against the rubric |

Miss a day: catch up at **one** lesson. Do not stack three as punishment.

## How you gauge other people

Use the [Problem Fluency Rubric](docs/rubric.md).

- **Understand columns** → how well they understand the problem
- **Plan + Execute columns** → how well they could solve it, or did solve it (process, not luck)
- **Review + hygiene** → whether learning will stick

Score artefacts and observable behaviour. Do not score character.  
Bands: theatre / unframed → partial / heroics → competent framing → disciplined → facilitator-grade.

Coaching questions: [docs/coaching-questions.md](docs/coaching-questions.md). Ask them. Do not donate the fix.

## Non-goals

- Not clinical psychology or therapy
- Not a blame process
- Not a dump of live homework, scored initials, or workplace secrets
